display:
block, inline, inline-block, none, flex, grid


block:
will make the elment take its parents full width

inline:
will make the elment takes as much space as neaded only

inline-block:
best of the two worlds
elment is inline but we can set a width and height to it normally
elments can have margins top and bottom

none:
totally removes the elment from the dom "html document in the brouser"

flex:
most common way to organize component in the page.
convert the elment in to flex container.

grid:
used for layout systems.
